Steal Animal Egg is a new animal tycoon from Sillytung Studios (it came out in September 2026). I played my first few minutes and recorded it, so everything below is what actually happened, with screenshots from that run.

## Redeem the code first

You start with $0, and your first pen upgrade costs $500, so grab the free cash before you do anything else.

1. Click the red **Shop** button (the basket) on the left side of the screen.
2. Scroll all the way down the shop, past the eggs, cash packs, run speed and potions, until you reach the **Codes** box.
3. Type **DEMONIC** into the Enter Code box and press **Redeem**.

![The Codes box at the bottom of the Shop after redeeming DEMONIC. The box shows +$1K! and the cash counter jumps to $1,000.](steal-animal-egg-code-redeemed.webp)

The box says **+$1K!** and your cash goes from $0 to $1,000 straight away. Codes are case sensitive, so type them in capitals like above.

## How the game works

The game's own description sums it up: take eggs from animals, hatch them into pets, and your pets earn you money. You can also eat food and steal from other players. The loop looks like this:

1. Walk to a giant animal and pick up one of its eggs.
2. Run back to your pen before the animal catches you.
3. The egg hatches into a pet that earns cash every second.
4. Spend the cash on pen upgrades and food, then go steal a better egg.

## Stealing your first egg

The easiest place to start is the **Duck Pond**. The duck is huge but the eggs lie on the grass around it.

![The Duck Pond. The giant duck sits in the middle and its eggs are on the grass around it.](steal-animal-egg-duck-pond.webp)

Walk up to an egg to pick it up. As soon as you have it, the screen turns red, **RUN!!** pops up and the animal comes after you. There's a **Drop** button if you need to let go of the egg.

![Carrying an egg while the screen flashes red and RUN!! shows at the top.](steal-animal-egg-run.webp)

Once you're clear, the game tells you to **Go to your Pen!** and points the way. Just keep running until you reach it.

![Running back with the egg while the top of the screen says Go to your Pen!](steal-animal-egg-go-to-pen.webp)

## Hatching and your pen

Put the egg in your pen and it hatches into a pet. My first one was a **Mini Duck**. From then on the pet earns cash every second, even while you're off stealing the next egg.

![The first egg hatching in the pen, with sparkles flying out.](steal-animal-egg-hatch.webp)

The sign in your pen shows the next **Upgrade Pen** level and its price. Level 2 to Level 3 cost **$500** for me, which is exactly why the code cash helps: you can buy it right away instead of waiting.

![The Upgrade Pen sign: Level 2 to Level 3 for $500.](steal-animal-egg-upgrade-pen.webp)

## Food and the table

After your first pet, the game walks you through buying a **table**, sitting at it and ordering food. After I ordered food, a **(x2)** showed up next to the number at the bottom left, so it's worth doing as soon as the game offers it.

![Sitting at the table with the Order button on the left.](steal-animal-egg-order-food.webp)

Keep stealing eggs, hatching them and upgrading the pen. Bigger animals have bigger eggs, and eggs can roll different sizes and mutations, so the rare ones are worth the risk of getting chased.

![Later on: a giant egg by the pens and the Eat! prompt at the top of the screen.](steal-animal-egg-eat.webp)

## Quick tips

- **Redeem codes before your first upgrade.** $1,000 from DEMONIC covers the first $500 pen upgrade twice over.
- **Start with the Duck Pond.** It's close to spawn and a good place to learn the steal and run.
- **Don't panic on RUN!!** Keep moving towards your pen. Only use Drop if you really can't escape.
- **Check the Index.** The Index button on the left shows every animal you can collect and has rewards to claim.
