I climbed BedWars Ranked in solo queue from Bronze all the way to Nightmare. Ranked changes almost every season, though, so this post starts with how it works right now. My own climb comes after that.

Everything below is for **Season 17**, which started with the August 21, 2026 update. It was checked against the BedWars wiki's update pages at the end of September 2026. BedWars gets small updates most weeks, so numbers can shift in the middle of a season.

## How BedWars Ranked works right now

### Who can play

You need an account that's at least **7 days old** and at **Player Level 20** or higher. The level requirement used to be 10, until the January 3, 2025 update raised it.

### The 7 ranks

Every rank below Nightmare is split into divisions, like Gold 1, Gold 2 and so on. It takes **100 RP** (Rank Points) to move up one division.

| Rank | Good to know |
|---|---|
| Bronze | The lowest rank. Placements can start you here |
| Silver | Placements can start you here too |
| Gold | Gold 4 is the highest rank placements can give you. First rank that earns a Victorious skin |
| Platinum | Can party with Gold, Platinum and Diamond players |
| Diamond | Diamond 4 came back in September 2025. Can party with Platinum up to Nightmare. Rank decay applies from Diamond 1 up |
| Emerald | Added in February 2023, with 4 divisions since October 10, 2025. Emerald 1 and up got harder to reach on May 22, 2026 |
| Nightmare | One tier, no divisions |

### Placements

If you haven't played Ranked yet this season, the wiki says you play **5 placement matches**, and they can put you anywhere from Bronze 1 to Gold 4. During placements you can only queue with Bronze to Gold players.

That 5-match rule was written before the RP changes of 2025 and 2026, so it may not be exact anymore.

### How RP works

A hidden **Matchmaking Rating** (MMR) decides how much RP you win or lose each match. Since October 10, 2025, the change scales with the gap between your visible rank and that hidden rating, and the biggest possible swing is different for each rank. If your rank is well below your real skill, you'll climb faster.

The May 22, 2026 update made beating a higher-ranked team worth more RP. The same update fixed RP gains and losses that weren't consistent.

### Rank decay

Season 15 (January 23, 2026) removed **demotion shields** and **rank skipping**, and it added **rank decay**. From Diamond 1 up, you lose 50 RP for every week you don't play at least one Ranked match.

Decay was switched off for 2 weeks when Season 16 started on May 8, 2026. The patch notes don't say clearly if it's back on in Season 17, so play one match a week and you're safe either way.

### 5v5 matches and kit bans

Season 15 was played in Squads. Season 16 moved Ranked back to **5v5** and brought back bed shields, and Season 17 is still 5v5: an August 26, 2026 fix stopped the game from cancelling 4v5 matches.

Every match opens with a draft:

1. Each team has **20 seconds** to vote on **2 kits to ban**. If your team doesn't agree, a random wheel picks.
2. Then everyone picks a kit without seeing what the other team took.

Limited-time items are turned off in Ranked.

Long games can't drag on forever. Ranked got map decay on January 27, 2026. In other modes it kicks in around 22 minutes: the backs of the bases go first, then the rest of the map breaks down over roughly 10 minutes.

Don't leave before the match starts. The first time, you get a warning. Keep doing it and you'll get timed out of Ranked.

### Ranked Blitz (new in Season 17)

The August 21, 2026 update also added **Ranked Blitz: One in the Chamber**. The regular One in the Chamber mode has no teams. Everyone spawns with a headhunter bow that deals 999 damage and just one arrow, each kill gives you another arrow, and the first player to 30 kills wins.

The wiki doesn't have a page for the ranked version yet, so it isn't clear if it changes those rules or has its own ladder.

### Seasons and rewards

Ranks have reset at the end of every season since Season 4 (March 25, 2022). Seasons 15 and 16 each lasted 15 weeks, and Seasons 14 and 15 both got a one-week extension near the end. The wiki didn't list an end date for Season 17 at the end of September.

What you get when a season ends:

- A **title** with your rank and the season.
- From **Gold** up, a **Victorious kit skin** in your rank's version (Gold, Platinum, Diamond, Emerald or Nightmare). Higher ranks get the lower versions too.
- An **exclusive emote** if you're in the global top 200. It's taken away if you drop out.

Since May 22, 2026, rewards are based on the **highest rank you reached** during the season, not where you're sitting when it ends. The kit that gets the Victorious skin is picked by a vote in the official BedWars Discord. Silas won the Season 16 vote with 52.2% of 6,704 votes.

## Before your first Ranked match

1. Get to **Player Level 20** on an account that's at least 7 days old.
2. Queuing with friends? Everyone's rank has to be in range, or the party won't queue.
3. Pick your 2 bans before the draft. The vote only lasts 20 seconds.
4. Don't leave before the match starts.
5. From Diamond 1 up, play at least one match a week.
