The Fisherman and the Trapper are back in BedWars. Both kits first came out on October 23, 2021 as Season 2 Battle Pass rewards: the Trapper at level 3 (the lowest level for a kit in any Battle Pass) and the Fisherman at level 25. Once Season 2 ended on December 17, 2021, nobody could get them anymore.

That changed on Friday, September 25, 2026. The official announcement was titled "Trapper & Fisherman Return!", and both kits came back reworked.

**Version note:** everything below is from the September 25, 2026 update and the Trapper balance patch that followed on September 26. A Balance Changes update was scheduled for October 2, with no details shared ahead of time.

## How to get the Fisherman and Trapper

Both kits are sold as bundles in the shop:

- **Trapper Bundle:** the Trapper kit plus the Pearlescent Trapper skin.
- **Fisherman Bundle:** the Fisherman kit plus the Spirit Fisherman skin.

If you unlocked either kit in the original Season 2 Battle Pass, you also get that kit's OG Chroma skin. BedWars did the same when Jack came back in July, and for Jade and Bounty Hunter in August.

The official notes don't say if the bundles are limited-time, or if the kits will be sold for BedCoins later.

## How the Trapper works now

The Trapper is a Defender kit. The biggest change is that you don't put traps on the ground anymore. You throw them, and they stick to floors, walls and ceilings, so an ambush can go almost anywhere.

Only the Trapper can buy traps, and there are three:

| Trap | Cost | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| Snap Trap | 12 iron | Roots and grounds the enemy who sets it off, and marks them |
| Venom Trap | 16 iron | Bursts when an enemy gets close and poisons nearby targets |
| Explosive Trap | 24 iron | Goes off when you detonate it, damaging enemies and nearby blocks |

What each trap does comes from the official notes. The prices come from the community BedWars wiki.

### Snap Trap changes

The Snap Trap changed the most. According to the wiki:

- You can have 8 out at once, down from 18. Placing a 9th removes your oldest one.
- The price dropped from 24 iron to 12.
- It no longer deals 5 damage when it goes off.
- The grounded effect lasts 3 seconds instead of 7.

Two more changes are in the official September 26 notes. An enemy who triggers a Snap Trap can't use items for 0.5 seconds. The Mark is also back, after the rework first took it out: for 5 seconds, your next direct hit on a marked enemy deals 60% more damage and 50% more horizontal knockback.

Nobody has posted how long the root lasts now. Before the rework, the wiki had it at 1.75 seconds.

### The September 26 Trapper patch

One day after the rework, the Trapper got an official balance patch. Besides the Snap Trap changes above:

- Throw range went up from 12 to 15 blocks.
- Detection height, trigger radius and effect areas all got bigger.
- Traps trigger more reliably around nearby blocks.
- On mobile, you can hold the throw button and drag to aim.
- The Detonate button greys out when no Explosive Traps are armed.

### Still not confirmed

There are no official numbers yet for Venom Trap poison or Explosive Trap damage. It's also unclear how many Explosive Traps you can have armed, and if throwing has a cooldown. Before the rework, the Trapper started each match with 3 free Snap Traps. Nothing says if that's still true.

## How the Fisherman works now

The Fisherman is an Economy kit. His old in-game description was just "Why fight when you can fish?" The new one keeps that line and adds Fish Coins, rod upgrades and bait.

A Fisherman match now goes like this:

1. You start with a fishing rod.
2. Fishing spots spawn at random around the map. Fishing in certain ponds gives better rewards.
3. Catches give resources, and sometimes Fish Coins. That's a new currency only the Fisherman uses.
4. You spend Fish Coins in the Item Shop on a better rod, which gives you a better shot at good loot.
5. Once you own the top rod, Fish Bait shows up in the shop. Each bait gets used up and gives you a chance to hook a Shark.

### Rod tiers

The wiki lists three rods:

| Rod | Cost |
|---|---|
| Beginner | Free at the start |
| Novice | 15 Fish Coins |
| Master | 35 Fish Coins |

The official notes say the Fisherman "now starts with a novice fishing rod", though. So either the names or the starting rod are off on one side.

Fish Coin numbers are messy right now. The wiki's Fish Coin page says a catch sometimes gives 1 to 2 coins along with the resources, but the Fisherman page says 1 to 10, depending on the fish and the pond. Some special fish only drop coins.

### Ponds and loot

The community wiki says there are 4 starter ponds, one at each diamond generator. After a rod upgrade, a darker pond shows up in the middle of the map with more red and golden fish. With the top rod, that pond also has a Shark in it. Bait makes the Shark easier to hook, and catching it doubles the special items you get. The wiki also says this pond moves every 2 to 3 catches.

Those details were added by players in the first days after the update, so they aren't confirmed.

The same page has a loot table. Rewards scale with match time and luck, and the page is flagged as needing cleanup, so these are rough:

| Pond | Iron | Diamonds | Emeralds |
|---|---|---|---|
| Beginner | 10 to 25 | 1 to 4 | 1 to 2 |
| Novice | 50 to 100 | 3 to 12 | 2 to 13 |
| Master | 100 to 200 | 8 to 15 | 10 to 15 |

Even the beginner ponds can drop a few special items, like obsidian, coral, blastproof ceramic and TNT. Novice ponds add a lot more, including siege TNT, telepearls, knockback fish, charge shields and a rocket launcher with rockets. The master pond gives about double.

The wiki also says the Fisherman is the only kit with access to the Rocket Launcher, Coral Blocks and the Charge Shield in normal matches.

Bait price, Shark odds and what a Shark actually gives you haven't been posted officially.

## What changed from the old kits

| | Before the rework | Now |
|---|---|---|
| Traps | Snap Traps placed on the ground | Three trap types, thrown |
| Snap Trap | 24 iron, 18 max, 7s grounded, 5 damage | 12 iron, 8 max, 3s grounded, no damage |
| Fishing rod | One rod, 40 iron from the Item Shop | Start with a rod, upgrade with Fish Coins |
| Where you fish | Into the void | Fishing spots and ponds |
| Bait and Sharks | None | Bait unlocks with the top rod |
