**Be The Biggest Streamer** is a new Roblox tycoon from the group Cool Game Only. You start with 0 viewers and $0, go live, play mini games on stream and try to grow into the biggest streamer on the server. It came out on **October 21, 2026**, it just got **Update 2**, and when I checked on November 17 it had about **15,000 people playing** and over **1.4 million visits**.

## Does Be The Biggest Streamer have codes?

**No, not yet.** There are no codes for Be The Biggest Streamer right now, and the game page doesn't mention any. If you find a site listing codes for it, they're made up.

What the game does have is free stuff without codes:

- **The Lucky Wheel** gives free rewards when you spin it.
- **Joining the Cool Game Only group** gives you an exclusive emote, according to the game page.
- **Events:** there's a world event every 20 minutes. When I played, an "Admin Abuse" event was running with **x2 money** and a **Luck Up** boost for 5 more days.

If codes get added in an update, I'll add them here and make a codes page for the game.

## How the game works

The whole game is a loop: go live, play a mini game well, gain viewers, earn money, then spend that money on better gear so you gain viewers even faster.

![Playing Flappy Egg on stream. The bar on the left shows my viewers (57 out of 60 for the next goal), the bottom shows my rank on the server, my money and how much I earn per second, and the chat on the right reacts to how I play. My screenshot.](btbs-flappy-egg.webp)

When you go live, the screen turns into a stream:

- **The mini game** is in the middle. The first one is **Flappy Egg**: tap to fly your egg between the pipes.
- **The viewer bar** on the left fills up as you play well. Mine said 57 out of 60, with +$6 for reaching the goal.
- **The bottom bar** shows your viewers, how long you've been live, your rank on the server and your money. I was **#9 of 11** with **$289**, earning **$2.3 per second** (doubled by the event).
- **The chat** on the right reacts to what you do. When I crashed with 0 points, it was full of "0 points lol" and crying emojis.
- **Hype Rush** at the top right is a bar that fills while you stream.
- **End Stream** takes you back to your house.

Your money keeps coming in while you're offline too, according to the game page, so it's worth going live before you leave.

## Unlocking new games

You don't play Flappy Egg forever. The game has **13 mini games**, and new ones unlock as your channel grows. My stream title showed what the next goal was: "NEW GAME at $150!"

![The moment Impossible Jump unlocked: "NEW GAME UNLOCKED! Try Impossible Jump!" with confetti. My screenshot.](btbs-impossible-jump.webp)

Once I reached it, the game unlocked **Impossible Jump**, where you tap to jump your egg over spikes. After that, my stream title changed to "Road to 100...", the next viewer goal.

## Your house, gear and pets

When you end a stream, you're back at your house. Two buttons at the top switch between **Home** and **Lobby**.

- **Gear:** a hint at the bottom of my screen said "Buy the Headset Mic at the GEAR shop". Better mic, camera and PC multiply your viewers.
- **Houses:** the **Next House** sign next to my house showed the next one, for **$1,200**.
- **Cars:** there was a van parked next to my house with a "x1 Money" boost on it.
- **Pets:** you hatch eggs to get pets, and there are 100+ of them, from Common to Secret. The **Index** button on the left (the book with a paw print) is where you see them.
- **Invite:** brings friends into your server. Servers hold up to 8 players.

The sign in front of my house shows your name and your viewer count, so other players can see how big your stream is.

## Tips for new players

1. **Unlock the next game first.** Watch your stream title. It tells you the next goal, like "NEW GAME at $150!".
2. **Play during events.** An x2 money event doubles everything you earn on stream.
3. **Buy the Headset Mic early.** The game points you to it first, and gear multiplies your viewers.
4. **Go live before you log off,** since you keep earning money while you're offline.
5. **Spin the Lucky Wheel** whenever it's free.
