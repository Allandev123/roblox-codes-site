In [+1 Nose to Escape](/guides/plus-1-nose-to-escape/) there are two things you buy with Wins to train faster: **noses** and **Blooms**. This page lists all of them, using the numbers from the game's settings. I made the game, so these are the real values, not estimates.

## How noses work

Noses are in the **Nose Shop** in the lobby. Walk onto a nose's pad to buy it or put it on. The pads are colour-coded:
- **White:** the nose you're wearing.
- **Blue:** noses you already own.
- **Green:** the next one, and you can afford it.
- **Red:** the next one, but you need more Wins.
- **Dark:** noses further down the line.

Each nose sets how much Nose you get per second of training. Rebirths, Blooms, boosts and treadmills all multiply that number, so a better nose makes everything else better too. Noses unlock **in order**, so you can't skip ahead to an expensive one.

The **Cosmic** nose (+250) isn't on this list because it isn't bought with Wins. It's a Robux gamepass on its own podium at the end of the shop. You don't need it to finish the game.

The later noses also look different. Ruby, Emerald and Void glow, Crystal, Lightning, Golden and Galaxy sparkle, Lava and Fire burn, and Rainbow cycles through every colour.

## Which nose to buy next

Buy the next nose as soon as you can afford it. The first few cost almost nothing next to what you earn on the stages, and Carrot doubles your Nose for just 1 Win. Later, the price goes up faster than the gain, so it's worth mixing in Blooms.

## Blooms

Blooms are flowers that grow along your nose while it's stretched, so everyone can see which one you have. You buy them in the **BLOOMS** menu with Wins, and each one multiplies your Nose. You use one at a time, and you keep every Bloom you buy.

| Bloom | Cost (Wins) | Nose multiplier |
|---|---|---|
| Daisies | 10 | 1.2x |
| Roses | 50 | 1.4x |
| Candy | 250 | 1.7x |
| Crystals | 1,200 | 2x |
| Fire | 8,000 | 2.5x |
| Lightning | 40,000 | 3x |
| Galaxy | 400,000 | 4x |

Blooms can also be bought with Robux, but every one of them can be earned with Wins just by playing.

## Noses, Blooms and rebirths together

Your Nose per second is roughly: your nose's gain, times your Bloom, times your rebirth multiplier, times your treadmill and any boosts. That's why a player with the Emerald nose, Crystals Bloom and a few rebirths can out-train someone with a much more expensive nose and nothing else. The [rebirth guide](/guides/plus-1-nose-to-escape-rebirths/) covers the rebirth part.

A rebirth never takes away your noses or Blooms. You keep them for good.
