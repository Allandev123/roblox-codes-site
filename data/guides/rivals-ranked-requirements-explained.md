Ranked in RIVALS got a lot harder to get into this year. If the button is locked for you, that's normal: the requirements went up in April, and in September the devs even posted advice for players who suddenly lost access.

This guide is based on Season 3 ("The Fame Season") up to Update 23 (September 18, 2026). Season 4 could be close, so double check rewards in-game.

## Ranked requirements in Season 3

You need all five of these:

| Requirement | What it means |
|---|---|
| 10 duels | Play 10 standard duels. Wins and losses both count. |
| Level 100 | Your level in RIVALS. |
| 30 tasks | Finish at least 30 tasks. |
| Account age | Your Roblox account is at least 14 days old. |
| Verification | Your Roblox account is verified. |

Level 100 and verification came with Update 20 (April 24, 2026), which started Season 3. The task and account age rules come from the community wiki and other guides, not the patch notes. If anything here doesn't match the locked screen in your game, trust the game.

### Getting to Level 100

Below Level 100 you don't earn XP in Arcade Servers (Free For All, Team Deathmatch and Gun Game). If you're grinding levels, stick to regular duels.

Task progress in Arcade Servers was switched off for a while in Update 21. Update 22 turned it back on.

### Verification (ask a parent first)

Update 22 (September 4, 2026) added "even stricter verification on Roblox's end". The devs said anyone who suddenly lost Ranked access should verify more of their Roblox account, like an email, a phone number or an age check.

Exactly which one you need isn't clear, and the wiki says email hasn't been working because of a Roblox bug. Check the locked Ranked screen to see what your account is missing.

This is done in your Roblox account settings, not in RIVALS. **Ask a parent before adding a phone number, doing an ID or age check, or changing any account settings.**

Roblox's age check uses a quick selfie with your device's camera, and Roblox says the images are deleted right after they're processed. If it gets your age wrong, a parent can fix it through Parental Controls. Roblox has required this check for chat since January 2026, so you may have done it already.

### Region lock

Update 22 also made Ranked region locked, so you mostly get duels in your own region. Some regions may have longer queues at certain times of day. Update 23 only undid the ELO changes, so the lock should still be on.

## Formats and match rules

Ranked has three formats: 1v1, 2v2 and 3v3. Each has its own pool of competitive maps. It also plays a bit differently from normal duels:

- Each team bans one map and two weapons before the match.
- The default loadout (Assault Rifle, Handgun, Fists, Grenade) can't be banned unless everyone owns more than four weapons in that category.
- Third person is off, and aim assist is 50% weaker.
- Auto Shoot is locked to "Dynamic".
- You can swap your loadout twice per duel at most.
- Your win streak is safe while you play Ranked.

## Ranks and ELO

Your rank comes from your ELO. Each rank below Nemesis has three divisions (I is the lowest).

| Rank | I | II | III |
|---|---|---|---|
| Bronze | 0 | 200 | 400 |
| Silver | 600 | 800 | 1,000 |
| Gold | 1,200 | 1,400 | 1,600 |
| Platinum | 1,800 | 2,000 | 2,200 |
| Diamond | 2,400 | 2,600 | 2,800 |
| Onyx | 3,000 | 3,200 | 3,400 |
| Nemesis | 3,600+ | | |

Unranked is below 0. Archnemesis isn't an ELO number: it's the top 200 players on the leaderboard.

The ELO you win or lose depends on who you play. Beat someone much lower and you get very little. Lose to them and it hurts. The wiki's example is a Platinum III beating a Silver II for about +3, or losing about 38 to 40.

Since Update 21 (June 26, 2026), enemy ranks stay hidden until the duel ends. The devs did it so players don't mentally give up at the start. Private-server Ranked duels have shown enemy ranks since Update 22.

### The ELO change that got reverted

Update 22 tried a new ELO formula. Players above 3,600 were no longer treated as equal, and winning a very lopsided match could give +0 ELO. If that happened, you got a "Stomp" charm for a random weapon instead.

Two weeks later, Update 23 undid it. The devs said changing ELO mid-season "was not the right move" and that the changes should come back next season, when everyone starts fresh.

### Placements

Your rank shows after 10 placement matches. Where you land depends on how well you play and your rank last season, not only on wins. The wiki says your level plays a part too. Update 21 changed placements so returning players land at a more reasonable ELO.

The wiki lists Diamond I as the highest possible placement, though that may be out of date.

## Shields, decay and leaving

**ELO Shields.** After placements you get one free shield per day. It stops ELO loss for one match but gets used up even if you win. Shields don't stack and can't be bought. From Platinum I (1,800 ELO) up, you don't get daily shields.

**Decay.** This only affects Onyx I and higher. Go 7 days without a Ranked match and you lose 100 ELO per day from day 8. It stops when you play Ranked again or drop to 3,000 ELO (Onyx I).

**Leaving.** Leaving a match in progress is an automatic loss plus a 15-minute matchmaking lockout, whatever the reason.

**Parties.** Everyone in a party has to be within 4 divisions (800 ELO) of each other. A Platinum I can queue with anyone from Silver III to Diamond II.

## Season rewards and Glory

When a Ranked season ends, ranks reset for everyone who finished placements. You get Glory based on your final ELO and total wins, plus a season charm for your final rank.

The top 200 (Archnemesis) also get a Mythical Arch skin. Past ones were the Arch Katana, Arch Crossbow and Arch Molotov. The wiki lists the Arch Uzi as the Season 4 one, for players who finish Season 3 in the top 200.

Ranked Shop items can only be bought with Glory. [Keys](/guides/rivals-how-to-get-keys/) and other currencies won't work there.

The community wiki uses this formula:

**Glory = 1,250 × (Ranked wins ÷ 250) + 1,250 × (final ELO ÷ 3,600)**

That part is capped at 2,500. The Ranked Contract adds up to 500 more, so the max is 3,000 per season. It isn't an official dev number, so it may change.

Season 3's Ranked Contract rewards Ranked wins with a free universal charm, wrap, finisher, emote and some Glory. Its final tier, at 200 wins, is the Spy Gloves skin for Fists.

### Ranked Shop prices

| Item | Glory |
|---|---|
| Emoji: Imp charm | 250 |
| Impaled finisher | 500 |
| Flex emote | 1,000 |
| Black Glass wrap | 2,000 |

Every weapon also has a Glory Track you buy in order: Glory Coin charm (100), Glorious wrap (200), For Glory finisher (300), then the Glorious skin for that weapon (400). A full track is 1,000 Glory. Utilities that don't deal damage, like the Medkit and Grappler, skip the finisher. Update 22 added the Glorious Wildcat.

## What's next for Ranked

As of late September 2026, Season 4 had no announced date. In June the devs admitted Ranked "has its issues" and promised work on matchmaking, ELO gain and the Glory Shop. They said most of those fixes wouldn't make the next update but would be ready in time for Season 4, so expect some of this guide to change.
