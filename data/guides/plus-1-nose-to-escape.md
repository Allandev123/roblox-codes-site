I'm Allan, and I run this site. I'm also making a Roblox game called **+1 Nose to Escape**. It isn't out yet, so this page explains what it is, how it plays and which codes will work on launch day. Since I'm the one making it, everything here comes straight from the game's own settings, not from guesses.

> Heads up: the game is still being tested. Numbers can change before launch, and I'll update these guides when they do.

## What kind of game is it?

It's a "+1" game, the kind where you grow a stat a little at a time and use it to get further. In this one the stat is your nose. You're Pinocchio, and every lie makes your nose longer.

![The lobby: the Nose Shop on the left, the treadmills on the right, and the gate to Stage 1 at the end of the runway.](nose-lobby.webp)

The map is a lava canyon split into stages. Each stage is a row of stone platforms with lava in between, and the gaps get wider as you go. You get across by stretching your nose to the next platform and letting it pull you over.

## How the nose works

This is the part I spent the most time on, because I wanted it to feel different from other +1 games.

![Riding my nose across a gap on Stage 3. The balloons on the left are worth bonus Wins.](nose-ride.webp)

- **Hold** (click, Space, or the NOSE button on a phone) and Pinocchio starts telling lies like "I never lie!" or "I did my homework!". While you hold, your wooden nose grows toward where your camera is looking.
- **Let go** and the nose snaps back, yanking you face-first to the tip.
- If the tip is stuck in a floor, you land there. If it's stuck in a wall, you pull yourself up onto the top. If you let go in mid-air, you drop.

How far your nose can reach depends on your Level. At Level 1 it reaches 36 studs, and every Level adds 7 more. So the whole game is about growing your Level until your nose can reach the next platform.

## How you get stronger

You grow your nose on the treadmills in the lobby. Every second on a treadmill gives you Nose, and your total Nose is also what fills your Level bar. Tissue boxes pop up on screen while you train. Tap one before it shrinks away and your next gain is worth up to 1.5 times more.

![Training on a treadmill. The little tissue boxes that pop up give a bonus if you tap them in time.](nose-training.webp)

When you cross a stage and step on its **win pad**, you get **Wins**. Wins buy new noses in the Nose Shop, and every new nose gives more Nose per second. Later on you **rebirth**, which starts your Level again but makes all your future Nose worth more for good.

## Launch codes

The game has a code box, and these are the codes I've set up for launch:

| Code | What it gives |
|---|---|
| `RELEASE` | 3 wheel spins and 2x Nose for 15 minutes |
| `NOSE` | 25,000 Nose |
| `GROUP` | 2 wheel spins (you need to be in the group) |

To use one, press the **gear button in the top right corner**, find the **Codes** row in the Settings window, type the code and redeem it. Capital letters and spaces don't matter in this game, so `release` works too. Each code works once per account.

The `GROUP` code only works if you've joined the game's Roblox group, [Group insane](https://www.roblox.com/communities/16078632). Group members also get two other things: **AUTO PLAY** for free, and an extra free wheel spin every day.

## Free rewards without codes

I didn't want codes to be the only free stuff, so there's a lot to collect just by playing:

![The Spin the Wheel board in the lobby, next to the Top Nose leaderboard.](nose-wheel.webp)

- **Daily rewards** for 7 days in a row, like 2x Nose boosts and free rebirths.
- **Playtime gifts** that unlock as you play: 9 of them, from 2 to 90 minutes.
- **The wheel**: one free spin a day, and the top prize is 250 Wins.
- **3 daily quests**, like "Stretch your nose 40 times", and a bonus of 2 spins for finishing all three.
- **A free chest** in the lobby that refills every 15 minutes.
- **Like goals**: when the game reaches a like goal (25, 50, 100 likes and up), everyone gets free spins.
- **Friends**: every friend in your server gives +10% Nose, up to +50%.

## What's coming later

Right now there's one world, Pinocchio Village. In the lobby you'll see a glowing "Coming soon" portal. That's where the next worlds will go: **Whale Belly** and then **Toy Island**, each unlocked after a certain number of rebirths.
