RIVALS has a lot of settings now, and most of them sit in tabs you'd never open by accident. This post covers the ones that change how the game feels: sensitivity, field of view and the crosshair editor. It also shows how to copy someone's whole setup in one go. My own settings are further down.

Everything here is based on Update 23 (September 2026). The Settings page was rebuilt in Update 18 (the Labor of Love update, February 2026), and Updates 19 to 21 added most of the newer crosshair and hitmarker options. Updates 22 and 23 were mostly fixes and weapon balance, so if an older guide uses names that don't match your screen, it's probably from before Update 18.

## Where everything is after Update 18

Update 18 added more tabs and sections, and lots of settings were renamed or moved. Many settings also depend on others now. Turn off Crosshair, for example, and every crosshair option under it disappears. Can't find something? Check that the setting above it is switched on.

The same update added **3 settings profiles** you can switch between. On mobile, each one is synced to one of your touch-button layouts.

## How to copy someone's settings

You can share a whole settings profile as a string of text, or just one section of it, like only the crosshair. To import one:

1. Open **Settings** and switch to a spare profile slot you don't mind overwriting.
2. Press the share button below profiles 1 to 3 (the one with an arrow and a tray).
3. Paste the full string into the box labelled **Paste someone else's settings here**.
4. Press **Import**, then try it in the Shooting Range.

A full string can change your sensitivity, camera, aiming, keybinds and crosshair all at once. That's why the spare slot matters. A crosshair-only string leaves the rest of your setup alone.

Two things catch people out. Import only accepts the string the game makes, so a typed list like "FOV: 97" has to be entered by hand. And it can't match your mouse DPI or Roblox's own camera and graphics settings, since those sit outside RIVALS.

To share yours, open the same popup and copy the string next to **Share your entire settings by copying this.** The line under it shares a single section.

## Sensitivity

There are three sliders: **Sensitivity** for normal looking around, **Sensitivity While Aiming** for non-scoped weapons, and **Sensitivity While Scoped**. Before Update 18 the aiming setting covered scoped weapons too. Now you can slow down scoped shots without touching anything else.

There's no single best number. The same RIVALS sensitivity feels completely different at 400, 800 or 1600 DPI, and Roblox's own camera sensitivity changes it again. Someone else's number only works if the rest of their setup matches yours.

To find your own:

1. Write down your Roblox camera sensitivity, mouse DPI, and all three RIVALS values.
2. In the Shooting Range, do a quick swipe of about 180 degrees and see if it feels controlled.
3. Track a moving target for 30 seconds.
4. Change one setting at a time, then test again.

## Field of view

There are two FOV sliders too: **Field Of View Non-Scoped Weapons** and **Field Of View Scoped Weapons**. Set the non-scoped one first, since that's what you see most. Then tune the scoped one on its own.

A wider FOV shows more of the map but makes enemies look smaller.

| FOV | How it plays |
|---|---|
| 90 to 95 | Calmer, bigger targets |
| 96 to 100 | Balanced (guides often suggest 97 or 100) |
| 101 to 110 | Wider view, smaller targets |

Some settings guides suggest 90 to 100 for 1v1 and 2v2, and 105 to 110 for TDM and FFA. It's also worth turning **Gameplay Effects** off, which keeps your FOV from shifting around mid-fight.

## The crosshair editor

| Option | What it does |
|---|---|
| Static | Turns off crosshair movement effects |
| Override Special Crosshairs | Uses your crosshair instead of some weapons' own |
| Rotation / Scale | Turns it, or makes it bigger or smaller |
| Show While Aiming | Keeps it on screen while aiming |
| Show While Inspecting | Keeps it on screen while inspecting (added in Update 21) |
| Bars | Colour, spacing, length, thickness, transparency, shape, each side on or off |
| Dot | Colour, thickness, transparency, shape |
| Circle | A ring around the dot |
| Outline | Colour, thickness, transparency, type |

Scoped weapons get their own options. **Scoped Red Dot** (Update 19) turns off the red dot on the Sniper and Crossbow. **Scoped Dot Color** recolours it, and **Scoped Bars Enabled** turns bars off while scoped (both Update 20).

Hitmarkers have Scale, Rotation, Transparency and Critical Transparency. Since Update 20, Crosshair Scale no longer resizes the hitmarker too (that was a bug).

Guides don't agree on Override Special Crosshairs, so try it both ways.

### Two starter crosshairs

RIVALS arenas are bright, and thin white crosshairs get lost on them. High-contrast colours like cyan, bright green or pink with a thin dark outline show up better.

**Classic cross.** This one gets copied a lot:

| Setting | Value |
|---|---|
| Static | On |
| Override Special Crosshairs | Off |
| Rotation | 0 |
| Bars | On, green #00ff21, Spacing 5, Length 10, Thickness 5, Transparency 0.5 |
| Dot | On, red #ff1435, Thickness 5, Transparency 0, Sharp |

**Simple dot.** Easier for beginners: a green #00ff00 dot at 5% transparency with a black #000000 outline at 80% transparency. If it's too big, lower Scale to about 125 to 150%.

If you already aim well, try a small cyan #00ffff cross and dot with a dark blue #000082 outline.

## Other settings worth changing

These are the values settings guides suggest most often:

- **Camera Shake:** Off.
- **Optimized performance settings:** On, for clarity and FPS. Update 22 fixed some buildings and paintings that Optimized Textures was missing.
- **Toggle Aim, Crouch and Sprint:** Off.
- **Auto Sprint, Easy Slide, Easy Quick Attack, Quick Attack Keeping:** On.
- **Keybinds:** Slide on Left Shift, Equip Next Weapon on Q.
- **HUD:** Damage flashing set to Outline, Padded HUD on.
- **Audio:** Since Update 18, Master Volume covers music too, and SFX Volume has its own slider.

## Mobile and controller

On mobile you can hide specific touch buttons, set transparency for all buttons or one at a time, and set camera sinking per button. The dedicated Slide button is hidden by default, so turn it on if you want it. **Opaque Buttons** (added in Update 20) keeps buttons solid while scoped.

Controller players got secondary binds in Update 18.

Ranked has its own rules: Third Person Mode is off, Aim Assist Strength is capped at 50%, and Auto Shoot Mode is locked to Dynamic. You can set **Auto Shoot Mode** to Dynamic in casual matches to practise under the same rules.
