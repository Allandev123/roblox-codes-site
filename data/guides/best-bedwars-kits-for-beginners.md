New to BedWars? The kit shop is full of 10,000 BedCoins price tags, but you can play plenty of kits without buying one. This guide covers the free kits first, then what the rest cost. Everything here is checked against the **September 25, 2026 update** (the one that brought back Fisherman and Trapper).

## The short version

- Every player gets **Isabel, Ragnar and Marcel** free, for good.
- **3 more kits are free each week**, and a 4th slot opens at Player Level 20.
- **Whisper** unlocks at Level 10 and **Taliyah** at Level 15.
- You can **rent a normal kit for 3 days** for 1,500 BedCoins.
- The cheapest kits to buy cost **10,000 BedCoins**.

## Free kits everyone gets

### Isabel, Ragnar and Marcel

These three are given to every player on first join. You can also play with no kit (the "None" option). The old "Random" option is gone.

**Isabel (Fighter)** has no ability button. While she holds a sword, she also holds a shield that blocks damage from the first hit. It scales with your best sword (8, 13, 20, then 30 damage), and the Emerald shield blocks the whole hit. It comes back every 10 seconds, even though the in-game text still says 30.

**Ragnar (Destroyer)** spawns with a wooden axe and gets discounts on axes and pickaxes. Press R for Berserker's Rage: 10 seconds of +50% block-breaking power, a little damage reduction and a few stacks of knockback resistance. It's been nerfed a lot since he came out, most recently on July 17, 2026, when the cooldown went from 30 to 33 seconds.

**Marcel (Defender)** is all about your bed. His Defense Scanner shows a blueprint around it and places the blocks instantly for iron: two layers of stone, then ceramic. He gets discounts on wool, stone brick, planks, blastproof ceramic and tesla traps (50 iron), and he can place 2 tesla traps instead of 1.

### The weekly free kit rotation

Every week, 3 kits are free for everyone, picked at random from the whole roster (the BedWars wiki counts 113 kits). Reach **Player Level 20** and you get a 4th free slot.

The rotation changes on **Fridays at 3:00 PM PT**, with the weekly update. This post went up on a Friday, so check the kit shop or that week's patch notes for the current kits. The notes list them and tag the Level 20 one.

If you like a rotation kit, you can buy it to keep after it leaves. The only exception is the odd battle pass kit that turns up.

### Kits you unlock by leveling up

These two can't be bought or gifted. You just need the Player Level.

- **Whisper (Level 10):** a Support kit. His Spirit Owl attaches to a teammate to heal them, speed them up and lift them out of the void.
- **Taliyah (Level 15):** an Economy kit. She hatches eggs into chickens, which you can sell for resources or throw at enemies.

| Player Level | Total XP needed | Reward |
|---|---|---|
| 10 | 75,000 | Whisper |
| 15 | 150,000 | Taliyah |
| 20 | 250,000 | 4th weekly free kit |

Every level-up also gives a Gold Lucky Crate. Your first 5,000 XP each day counts 2.5x, so a short session every day beats one long one. The daily cap is 52,000 XP.

### Events and battle passes

Sometimes every kit is free for a while. The July 31, 2026 update was an "All Free Kits Weekend".

Seasonal battle passes can include kits too. Zola was on both tracks of the Spring (Easter) 2026 pass, but the free Summer 2026 pass didn't have a kit. Halloween usually brings a special mode (see my look back at [Crypt's Trials from 2022](/blog/bedwars-crypts-trials-2022/)), so keep an eye on the October patch notes.

## What makes a kit good for beginners

BedWars sorts kits into 8 classes, which helps you pick a role. **Defender** kits protect your bed, **Economy** kits get extra resources and **Destroyer** kits break through bed defenses. The rest are Support, Movement, Tank, Fighter and Ranged.

Some kits also ask less of you. Isabel's shield works without pressing anything, and Barbarian (a 10,000 BedCoins kit) never has to shop for swords. A timed ability like Ragnar's rage is different: you have to pick the right moment.

## Rent a kit before you buy it

A kit is a big chunk of BedCoins, so try it first. Renting lasts **3 days**:

| Kit type | 3-day rental |
|---|---|
| Normal kit | 1,500 BedCoins |
| Battle pass kit | 2,000 BedCoins |
| Holiday bundle kit | 3,000 BedCoins |

Kit Rental Tickets from Lucky Crates, events and battle passes do the same job without spending BedCoins. Most of them only work on one type of kit (a Fighter ticket rents a Fighter kit, for example).

Also, finish the in-game **Tutorial**. You get the "Scholar" title and a **Beginner Kit Rental Ticket**, which rents one kit from a short list for a full week. Barbarian, Builder and Archer are on it.

## What to buy first: BedCoins prices

| Price | Which kits |
|---|---|
| 10,000 BedCoins | Standard kits (the wiki lists 48 at this price). The Season 10 battle pass kits (Agni, Styx, Nyoka, Bekzat and Hephaestus) cost the same. |
| 12,000 BedCoins | Recently added kits, like Zola |
| 25,000 BedCoins | Old battle pass kits that came back, like Warrior, Jack, Jade and Bounty Hunter |
| Not for BedCoins | Limited bundle-only kits, and pre-Season 10 battle pass kits that haven't come back |

Some simple kits from the 10,000 tier:

- **Barbarian (Fighter):** hitting enemies fills a rage bar that upgrades his sword, ending with the Rageblade. He loses rage when he dies, but his sword never goes back down.
- **Builder (Defender):** starts with 8 stone bricks, plus a hammer that gives blocks extra health and blast protection.
- **Archer (Ranged):** 12.5% extra projectile damage, headshots with any projectile, and the Tactical Crossbow.
- **Baker (Support):** health apples, speed pies and a knockback baguette.
- **Farmer Cletus (Economy):** crops that grow diamonds and emeralds.
- **Miner (Economy):** enemies he kills turn to stone, and he mines them for resources. He can't buy axes.

Why do some kits cost 25,000, 2.5 times the standard price? They're old battle pass kits. Warrior, Jack, Jade and Bounty Hunter each came back in a limited bundle sold in the shop first, then went on sale for 25,000 BedCoins two or three weeks later. Raven returned the same way on August 28, 2026, and Fisherman and Trapper on September 25. Check the kit shop to see if they've reached the 25,000 tier yet.

## How to get BedCoins now

Normal matches stopped giving BedCoins on November 7, 2025. These still do:

- Daily and weekly missions
- The AFK Area (60 BedCoins per hour)
- Lucky Crates and battle pass levels
- Tournaments, events and the clan shop

At 60 an hour, the AFK Area alone would need about 167 hours for one 10,000 kit, so missions matter. Your balance caps at 50,000 BedCoins. Lucky Crates can also drop a whole kit, but that's rare.

A handful of kits (Barbarian and Whisper among them) have **Kit Contracts**, reward tracks you fill by playing that kit. The first half is free. The second half costs 5,000 BedCoins, half the price of a standard kit.

## Quick checklist

1. Play Isabel, Ragnar and Marcel.
2. Finish the Tutorial for a week-long kit rental.
3. Check the free kits every Friday after 3:00 PM PT.
4. Play a little every day to reach Levels 10 and 15.
5. Rent before you spend 10,000 BedCoins.

Kit prices and free kits change with updates, and this guide is based on the September 25, 2026 update. If something's different in your game, [let me know](/contact/).
