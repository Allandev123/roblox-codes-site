BedWars has one rule that decides most games, and the official game description puts it simply: "Protect your bed. Once it's gone, you can no longer respawn!" Almost every beginner mistake below comes back to that bed, or to the iron and diamonds you spend keeping it alive.

The numbers here are from **Ranked Season 17**, which started on August 21, 2026, with Item Shop prices from late September 2026. Ranked has been 5v5 again since May 8, when Season 16 began. BedWars updates every Friday, so a price might have moved by the time you read this.

## 1. Wrapping the bed in one layer of wool

Wool is the cheapest block in the shop (8 iron for 16), so it's the easy pick for a new player's bed. It's also the weakest. Wool has only 8 health, shears cut through it fastest, and one or two TNT clears it.

The bed itself is blastproof, so explosives can't break it. Someone has to walk up and break it by hand, and axes are the tool for that. Your defense doesn't have to be unbreakable. It just has to slow them down until your team gets back.

| Block | Price | How it holds up |
|---|---|---|
| Wool | 8 iron for 16 | Weakest. Shears break it fastest, 1-2 TNT clears it |
| Oak Wood Planks | 14 iron for 8 | Axes break it. It burns, and 1-2 TNT clears it |
| Stone Brick | 40 iron for 16 | Needs a pickaxe. Takes 4 TNT |
| Blastproof Ceramic | 16 iron for 4 | Regular TNT and fireballs can't break it |
| Obsidian | 1 emerald for 4 | The strongest blastproof block in the shop. Needs a pickaxe |

> **[ALLAN]** Screenshot: the Item Shop blocks tab in a Ranked match, showing the wool, wood, stone brick, ceramic and obsidian prices.

Two things catch new players out. **Fireballs** (75 iron) set flammable blocks on fire, so a wood layer can burn away. **Siege TNT** (350 iron) is a much bigger blast, and it breaks ceramic too.

So a bed covered in one kind of block only needs one answer, like shears for wool or TNT for wood. Add a blastproof layer and regular TNT won't get through. Every extra layer is more time for your team.

Ranked beds do get some cover early on. They start with bed plating, and bed shields came back to Ranked on May 8. That only lasts the first few minutes, though, so build your defense before it runs out. And watch the clock: when the timer hits 20:00, every bed breaks on its own.

## 2. Not buying armor

If your first iron goes into a better sword instead of armor, think about what happens to each one when you die.

Armor stays with you. You respawn wearing the last armor you bought, and each purchase gives you the helmet, chestplate and boots in one go. Swords don't stay. Since August 16, 2024, your sword drops one tier when you die (Iron back to Stone, for example).

| Armor | Price | Damage reduction |
|---|---|---|
| Leather | 50 iron | 36% |
| Iron | 120 iron | 56% |
| Diamond | 8 emeralds | 76% |
| Emerald | 40 emeralds | 84% |

For comparison, the Iron Sword is 70 iron for 30 damage, up from 20 with the free Wood Sword. Iron armor cuts the damage you take by more than half, and you keep it.

## 3. Carrying all your resources into a fight

When someone kills you, they get every iron, diamond and emerald you're carrying. If you're in diamond armor or better, they get emeralds for the kill too. Running into a fight with a pile of unspent iron hands it to the other team if you lose.

Spend it before you leave base.

The generator matters too. Your team's iron generator stops once it's holding 100 iron at tier 1, 150 at tier 2 or 225 at tier 3, and it only restarts when someone picks it up. Iron sitting on a full generator is iron your team never gets.

### The 5v5 iron tax

Handing all your iron to one teammate early can backfire. In 5v5, if a player gets more than 50 iron from teammates in the first 4 minutes, iron prices in their shop double until the 4-minute mark (blocks, arrows and snowballs are spared until you buy armor). The tax was still in Ranked in 2025, when a February patch fixed how it counted chests, but no 2026 patch note confirms it works the same way in Season 17.

## 4. Forgetting team upgrades

Diamonds aren't just for you. The Team Upgrades NPC sells upgrades for the whole team, paid in diamonds:

| Upgrade | Tier 1 | Tier 2 | Tier 3 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Team Generator | 4 | 8 | 16 |
| Armor Protection | 4 | 8 | 20 |
| Damage | 5 | 10 | 18 |

Tier 3 Team Generator also drops emeralds at your base. The **Bed Alarm** costs only 2 diamonds and warns your team when enemies get near your bed. The wiki pages don't agree on the exact bonus each Armor Protection and Damage tier gives, so go by what the NPC shows.

A diamond generator drops 1 diamond every 40 seconds and stops at 5. At that rate, one generator on its own takes about 2 minutes 40 seconds to make the 4 diamonds for a first upgrade, and it makes nothing more once it's full and nobody's picking them up.

## 5. Bridging with no way back

Wool is the most-used block in the game, and bridging is a big reason why. A bridge is fine until it goes wrong, and the real problem is having no plan for that moment.

A fall into the void is almost always a death, and any fall higher than 5 blocks costs health too. The items that could save you both have catches:

- **Balloons** (2 emeralds) came back to the Item Shop on May 22, 2026. You can have 3 at most, they can pop when you take damage, and they always pop after 35 seconds. Since November 2024 they don't deploy by themselves near the void, so you have to use them in time.
- **Telepearls** (2 emeralds) teleport you when they hit a block. A pearl that falls into the void doesn't take you anywhere.

Balloons were a rotating item until this year, and limited-time items have been kept out of Ranked since 2022, so check your Ranked shop before you count on them.

Bridging also feels a lot harder when your game stutters. My [BedWars FPS post](/blog/bedwars-1000-fps-then-and-now/) covers what still helps in 2026.

## 6. Forgetting that the map falls apart late

Map Decay was added to Ranked on January 27, 2026, back when Ranked was Squads, so check that it still happens in your 5v5 games. It kicks in a little after beds break (the wiki puts the start around 21:00 on the timer). The backs of team bases go first, then the whole map slowly breaks apart until about 35:00. Every bed is already gone by then, so late fights happen on less ground with no respawns.

Health only starts coming back after 5 seconds out of combat. A fall right after a fight can finish you.

## Quick checklist

1. Use more than one kind of block on your bed, including a blastproof layer.
2. Buy armor early. You keep it when you die.
3. Spend iron before fights, and don't let the generator sit full.
4. Put diamonds into team upgrades, and grab the Bed Alarm.
5. Carry a backup when you bridge, if your Ranked shop has one.
6. Watch the clock: beds break at 20:00 and the map starts decaying soon after.
