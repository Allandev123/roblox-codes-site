Kit Fusion is the BedWars mode where you pick two kits and use both in the same match. The pairs that got the mode called "unplayable" were nerfed in 2026, so a lot of older combo advice is out of date.

Everything below is based on the BedWars patch notes up to the **September 4, 2026** update. Notes for the later September updates hadn't been written up yet at the time of writing. BedWars usually updates on Fridays, so numbers can move fast.

## How Kit Fusion works

Kit Fusion came out in the May 9, 2025 "Fusion" update, back when it was called Combined Kit. It got its own queue on June 6, 2025, and in August 2025 it replaced the old custom kit mode.

You'll find it through the Play button in the lobby. Matches run on the normal Squads setup (4 teams of 4). Once you load in, you get **30 seconds** to pick two kits, and then you can use both kits' items and abilities for the whole match.

Pick order matters. **Kit 1** decides which character you show up as, and **Kit 2** adds its skills and items on top. The wiki gives a clear example: put another kit first and Eldertree second, and you'll look like that first kit, not a giant tree.

### Fusions that don't work (or act weird)

Some pairs break because the two kits clash. A kit's special item might get lost when you die, or one kit's passive can mess up the other kit's items. A few known examples:

- **Merchant Marco + Warrior:** the armor discounts don't stack. You just pay the lower of the two prices.
- **Fortuna + another ability kit:** her card throw has been reported to land on the same key as the other kit's ability, so you'd have to tap the ability button by hand. Fortuna got a big rework on September 4, 2026, so this might behave differently now.
- **Ember + Lumen:** killing another Ember or Lumen maxes out both swords instantly.

The pick screen itself has been buggy too. Updates on January 16, February 13 and July 24, 2026 all fixed picks that didn't apply or got swapped for random kits. The wiki notes the July fix caused a new bug where the game thought you'd picked 3 or 4 kits.

## What got nerfed in 2026

The wiki names three fusions as the reason people called the mode unplayable: **Eldertree + Void Knight**, **Vanessa + Archer** and **Uma + Aery**. The main fix came in the March 6, 2026 **Void Knight Contract** update, which listed these Kit Fusion changes:

- Aery's spirit butterflies ramp up her damage more slowly.
- Zephyr's wind stack strike damage went from 3, 5, 7, 10, 12 down to 1, 2, 3, 4, 5.
- Eldertree gets less health from tree orbs.
- Void Knight's damage reduction is lower when he's fused with Eldertree.
- Styx + Marrow now puts you in skull form, then ghost mode if you die before being revived.

The same update fixed a bug where Vanessa's triple shot randomly fired supercharged with Archer's tactical crossbow or headhunter. A hotfix that week also fixed Void Knight + Eldertree taking negative damage from tier 2 onward.

Are these nerfs Fusion-only? The patch notes put them under Kit Fusion, and Eldertree's change history on the wiki says the orb nerf only applies in Kit Fusion. Aery's and Zephyr's normal kit histories don't list a March 6 change at all.

## Why the big three were so strong

### Eldertree + Void Knight

Eldertree grows by eating tree orbs, gaining max HP, size and knockback reduction, but it can't buy or wear armor. Void Knight doesn't use normal armor either. His noctium gear gives damage reduction (18% at tier I, up to 86% at the top tier) and it ignores armor penetration. Fused together, you got Eldertree's huge health pool with that damage reduction on top.

Both kits have been changed a lot in 2026:

| Date (2026) | Change |
|---|---|
| Jan 2 | Eldertree's vertical knockback reduction cut from 5% to 1% per orb (max 15%) |
| Jan 16 | Starting tree orbs cut from 5 to 4, and less max health per orb |
| Feb 13 | Void Knight's void shield no longer reduces fall damage |
| Mar 6 | Less orb health in Kit Fusion, and weaker Void Knight damage reduction in this fusion |
| Mar 13 | Buff: starting tree orbs raised from 4 to 6 |
| Apr 10 | Void Knight's tier III and IV gear upgrades now cost 12 and 48 emeralds (were 10 and 40) |
| May 8 | Buff: Eldertree picks up orbs just by walking over them |
| Sep 4 | Orbs spawn less near team bases, and early orbs give a bit less health |

### Vanessa + Archer

Vanessa's charged bow, crossbow or headhunter fires a triple shot, with the three arrows hitting for 100%, 133% and 58% damage. Archer brings the tactical crossbow and tactical headhunter, **+12.5% damage** on all projectiles, and headshots with most projectiles. Put together, every charged shot is three arrows with Archer's bonus behind them.

This pair never got a real stat nerf. The March change was a bug fix, and Vanessa's changes since then have been buffs: a small ranged knockback buff on March 27, 2026, and her arrows dropping from 12 to 10 iron on July 17.

If you're running a bow combo on PC, my [custom crosshair guide](/guides/roblox-custom-crosshair/) shows how to swap the default cursor for a crosshair.

### Uma + Aery

Aery gets 2 spirit butterflies per kill, and has to wait 25 seconds before the same player gives her more. Each butterfly adds damage for the rest of the match (with diminishing returns), but she loses part of her stacks when she dies. Since October 17, 2025, butterflies boost **all** her damage, not just her sword. That's why she pairs so well with kits that bring their own damage.

Uma's spirit staff throws beetles that deal damage over time to enemies or heal teammates. She can have up to 10 summon stones and beetles in total, gets a free stone every 20 seconds, and can buy more for 30 iron.

Uma hasn't been changed at all in 2026, so this pair only got weaker through the March butterfly nerf. Aery even got a small buff on April 10, when her butterfly stacks started counting for 80% on ranged weapons instead of 50%.

## Popular combos after the nerfs

| Combo | What it's good at |
|---|---|
| Trinity + Silas | Healing and shields for the team |
| Bounty Hunter + Baker | Turning bounty loot into healing |
| Trinity + Adetunde | Tanking |
| Yuzi + Yamini | Getting around the map fast |

### Trinity + Silas

YouTuber RealYoRyan tested this as a top healing fusion in a video on August 25, 2026. Trinity's Light Angel form gives shield to her and nearby teammates when she lands melee hits, up to 30 shield at a time. Silas adds an aura that heals or boosts damage, Triumph stacks that cut the damage he takes, and Press the Attack, which gives shield.

Silas got buffed on April 24, 2026. His max Triumph stacks went from 10 to 20, and during Press the Attack his auras now shield and boost every nearby teammate. That makes this a real team combo, not just a solo one.

### Bounty Hunter + Baker

AnWare called this possibly the best combo in the game in an August 27, 2026 video. Bounty Hunter gets iron and emeralds for each bounty, and since his August 14, 2026 rework he levels up as he finishes bounties, which raises the rewards (bounties don't pay diamonds anymore). Baker turns that loot into staying alive: health apples cost 40 iron and heal 20 HP, golden apples cost 3 emeralds and give healing, regen and 5% damage reduction, and speed pies cost 2 emeralds.

### Trinity + Adetunde

Milyon said this fusion makes you "unkillable" in a kit tier list from December 2025. Keep in mind that was before the 2026 changes. Adetunde's frosty hammer swings every 0.35 seconds, and its Shield upgrade gives shield on hits, which stacks with Trinity's Light Angel shield. Since April 10, 2026, the hammer's shield caps at 50 HP, so it's not as endless as it used to be.

### Yuzi + Yamini

Yuzi's dao dash lunges 8 blocks for 18 to 34 damage to everyone in front of her, with 4 charges and half the energy refunded when it hits. Yamini climbs walls and pounces 9 to 11 blocks for 10 damage plus decay. His pounce cooldown dropped to 5 seconds on January 2, 2026, the same update that gave him a stamina bar for climbing.

Some YouTube and TikTok clips call it the best combo, but no bigger source backs that up yet.
