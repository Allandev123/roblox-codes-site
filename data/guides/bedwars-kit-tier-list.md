## Season 17 ranked basics

Ranked is **5v5** again. It switched back from Squads when Season 16 started on May 8, and it's stayed that way. A fix on August 26 also stopped ranked from cancelling matches that ended up 4v5.

| Season | Dates |
|---|---|
| Season 15 | January 23 to May 8, 2026 |
| Season 16 | May 8 to August 21, 2026 |
| Season 17 | Started August 21, 2026 (no end date announced yet) |

Season 17 also added a new mode, **Ranked Blitz: One in the Chamber**. There isn't much info yet on how kits work there, so this list only covers normal 5v5 ranked.

A few rules before you queue:

- Your account has to be at least 7 days old and player level 20 or higher.
- Limited-time items are turned off, and Dodo Birds aren't allowed.
- Bed shields have been part of ranked since May 8, 2026.
- There are 7 ranks, from Bronze up to Nightmare. Nightmare only goes to the top 200 players on the leaderboard, and you need over 100 RP in Emerald to get it.

## How the draft and bans work

Every ranked match starts with a draft. Each team gets 20 seconds to ban two kits, picked by a team vote. If your team doesn't agree, a random wheel picks the ban for you. Then everyone picks a kit blind.

That's the catch with any ranked tier list. A top kit only helps you if it survives the bans, and since picks are blind, you can't wait to see the enemy's kits before you choose yours.

## What changed since July

This covers kit changes from July 3 to September 26, 2026. The September 11 and 18 updates don't have detailed patch notes, so a small change could be missing.

### Reworks and re-releases

**Trapper** (September 25): snap traps can now be thrown onto walls and ceilings. They cost 12 iron (was 24) and cap at 8 (was 18), and Trapper can also buy Venom Traps for 16 iron and Explosive Traps for 24. The rework took away the trap's 5 damage and its "marked" debuff. A hotfix the next day brought marked back, raised throw range from 12 to 15 blocks, and made snap traps block item use for 0.5 seconds.

**Fisherman** (September 25): fishing now drops Fish Coins, which buy rod upgrades (Novice for 15 coins, Master for 35). Better rods fish from better ponds, and the Master pond can hook a shark that doubles your special-item rewards.

**Raven** (August 28): stored poison energy powers two abilities. Avian Call sends homing ravens whose damage scales with your strongest sword, and Raven Storm (at full energy) hits up to 3 enemies in an area for 12 seconds. Kills also give you an extra raven. On September 4 the poison dropped from 5 to 4 damage per tick, and from 5 to 4 seconds.

**Bounty Hunter** (August 14): levels up by completing bounties. The new Focus ability locks onto your target for 15 seconds, and they take 10% more damage while other players take 10% less. Bounties don't pay out diamonds anymore, and since August 21 you can only get a new bounty every 20 seconds.

**Jade** (August 14): can buy 3 tiers of the Jade Hammer. Slam damage now depends on how far you fall and can pierce armor, but the hammer only reduces fall damage now instead of blocking all of it.

**Kaida** (August 7): Dragon Summon now aims at your cursor within 10 blocks, the Level IV radius went from 12 to 6 blocks, upgrades need more damaging casts, and the cooldown is 20 seconds (was 22). August 21 gave it faster charge times and a bigger max summon.
