## DOORS

DOORS is a horror game from the LSPLASH group, made by Lightning Splash with RediblesQW as co-developer. It came out on August 10, 2022, and it's passed 7 billion visits.

Its Roblox description opens with "A horror game involving doors." It also suggests going in without a guide, so I'll stick to the basics.

You go room by room through different areas (The Hotel, The Mines, The Outdoors and The Archives) and try to reach the end without getting killed by the entities that live there. Items like the lighter, flashlight, lockpick and vitamins help. You can buy them in the elevator shop before a run or at the Jeff Shop partway through.

### Playing DOORS with friends

Every run starts in the Lobby. Its regular elevators take 1 to 4 players, or you can make a custom elevator, set the player count and turn on a friends-only option so it's just your group going in.

Custom elevators go all the way up to 50 players. The DOORS wiki says the developers don't recommend groups that big, though, because there might not be enough hiding spots for everyone.

### What's new in DOORS this October

The Archives Update came out on August 28, 2026. It added two subfloors, The Archives and The Stairwell, with new entities and more than 10 new items. The official event text also says every subfloor stays free once you've discovered it.

For October, LSPLASH announced **ACCESS GRANTED**, a secret subfloor with new threats, items and achievements. It's free through the Lobby and was set to release on October 2, 2026. The official text doesn't call it a Halloween event.

The last DOORS Halloween event was the 2025 Trick or Treat Event (October 23 to December 1, 2025). In it, the World Lotus became the World Pumpkin, and you collected Candy to spend on cosmetics. The fan wiki says it came back as a Vision on February 5, 2026. No DOORS Halloween 2026 event was listed as of September 30.

## Dandy's World

Dandy's World is made by BlushCrunch Studio and was created by Qwelver. It came out in Alpha on June 14, 2024. The Roblox title still says Alpha, and the description warns you to expect bugs and shutdowns. It's passed 7 billion visits anyway.

The game calls itself a mascot horror survival game for multiple players. You play as Toons, team up to finish machines and go deeper into Gardenview Center. Each Toon has its own stats and abilities. You collect more of them, plus Trinkets that change your playstyle, and you can research the Twisteds, the monsters hunting you.

> **[ALLAN]** Screenshot: your team in a Dandy's World round, ideally at a machine or in the elevator.

### How a round works

On each floor, your team fills Machines by extracting Ichor. While you're on one, skill checks (quick-time events) can pop up. Fail one and nearby Twisteds get alerted.

Once every machine is full, Panic Mode starts. The Twisteds speed up and you have at least 30 seconds to get back to the elevator. Anyone who doesn't make it dies, no matter how many hearts they had left.

Between floors, Dandy's shop (where you trade Tapes for items) and a team card vote take turns. The deeper you go, the more machines and Twisteds show up.

### Playing Dandy's World with friends

A round starts from one of three elevator shafts in the Lobby. Each elevator holds 8 players, and there's a Matchmaking button if you're short on people. Private servers are free to host, and the fan wiki recommends one for your first time.

### Halloween 2026 in Dandy's World

This one's confirmed. BlushCrunch Studio's official Roblox event says the Halloween Event is back with new tricks and treats. It runs from October 2, 2026 at 19:00 UTC (3 PM EDT) to November 6, 2026 at 20:00 UTC, so it's still on at the end of October.

Before launch, the fan wiki expected these to return:

- The four Halloween Toons: Gourdy, Eclipse, Soulvester and Ribecca

- A Halloween Lobby with Gourdy's Store
- A limited-time Halloween Floor and a new Floor Event

Only the dates and the tricks and treats line came from the developers. The list was the wiki's guess based on past events.

## Evade

Evade is published by the Hexagon Development Community group and developed by OsterDog and Beefchoplets. Its alpha launched on June 10, 2022, the initial release came on August 4, 2022, and it's passed 9 billion visits.

The official description calls it an action parkour game where movement is how you survive the Nextbots, and says it's inspired by Garry's Mod. The Evade wiki says it's based on Nextbot Chase, a Garry's Mod addon. You run from nextbots and use items and movement tricks to last through each 3-minute round.

> **[ALLAN]** Screenshot: an Evade round with your friends, like a teammate carrying or reviving someone.

### Playing Evade with friends

Evade servers hold up to 15 players, though community servers and some gamemodes can be different.

Getting caught is where friends really help. When a nextbot catches you, you usually go down instead of dying right away (some nextbots kill you outright). Teammates can then carry you and revive you, either with the free revive button or with items like the Defibrillator and the Revive Grenade.

Since the Summer 2026 update on July 18, 2026, you can go down 3 times before you fully die. It used to be 2. If you're down and nobody can find you, press **T** on PC or console to whistle so your teammates can see where you are.

### Halloween 2026 in Evade

Evade has done Halloween events before. A Haunting All Hallow's Update ran from October 23 to November 21, 2022. The Halloween 2025 Update ran from October 24 to November 17, 2025, with a Halloween battle pass that used a currency called Bread.

For 2026, nothing was confirmed as of September 30. The Evade wiki lists "Halloween 2026" as the next update but gives no date, and there was no official Roblox event yet. Both past events started in the last week of October, but that's a pattern, not an announcement.
