**Survive Your Ideas** is one of the fastest growing new games on Roblox right now. It came out on **October 6, 2026**, and one month later it has more than **32,000 people playing at once** and over **3.3 million visits**. Here's what the game is, how a round works and whether it has any codes.

**When these facts are from:** the numbers and details below were checked on the game's Roblox page on **November 6, 2026**.

## What is Survive Your Ideas?

The idea is simple: **type any word, and it happens.** Every round, one random player in the server gets to write a word, and that word comes to life in the map. Write "tsunami" and a wave comes for everyone. Write "dinosaur", "nuke" or even "tacos", and that's what you have to survive.

The twist is that the words **stack up**. Each new word adds to the chaos that's already there, so the map gets harder and harder to survive. The **last player alive wins** the round.

![A player typing APOCALYPSE while meteors fall on a lava map, from the Survive Your Ideas game page.](survive-your-ideas-apocalypse.webp)

The thumbnails on the game page show some of the words players can try: a shark jumping out of the ground, an apocalypse with falling meteors, a giant 67, lava, a UFO, a tsunami and even "upside", which flips the whole map upside down.

## Why it's blowing up

Games where players control the chaos are easy to understand in a few seconds and fun to watch, which makes them perfect for short videos. Survive Your Ideas takes that further than most, since you never know what the next word will be. The game also gets updated often. It was last updated on November 5, and the description asks players to like and favorite the game to "get new words first", so new words seem to be added with updates.

## How to play

1. Search for **Survive Your Ideas** on Roblox, or find it on the Roblox page linked below.
2. Join a server and wait for the round to start.
3. When it's your turn, type a word and press **Send**.
4. Stay alive while the words pile up. The last one standing wins.
