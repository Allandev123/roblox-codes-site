Fisch got its **Boat Racing Update** on **September 26, 2026**. It adds boat races you can join on several islands, two new masteries and a handful of buffs. Fisch also turns two on **October 5**, and the developers have confirmed a limited 2nd Anniversary event. Here's what changed and what to try first.

> **[ALLAN]** Screenshot: a Race Leader NPC (Moosewood or Roslit Bay), plus one line on your first race.

## Boat races

Race Leader NPCs now stand on several islands, including **Moosewood** and **Roslit Bay**. Talk to one to start a race:

- **Practice races** are free (0 C$). Good for learning a track.
- **Competitive races** cost **100 C$** to enter.

In official races every boat gets the **same stats**, so the fastest driver wins, not the most expensive boat. Results go on a shared leaderboard.

## New masteries

Two new masteries were added: **Stellarwave Melody** and **Rod of the Singularity**. You find them in the progression menu, where each one shows its mastery quest.

> **[ALLAN]** Screenshot: the two new masteries in your progression menu.

## Buffs

- **Cinderstring:** Lure Speed 95% to 100%, Progress Speed 15% to 30%, Resilience 10% to 20%.
- **Infernal Mastery I:** 30 seconds to 60 seconds.
- **Five gauntlets** were buffed as well.

## Inventory changes

- Inventory and storage now cap at **32,000** item stacks.
- A new **Open All Crates** toggle opens your crates in one go instead of one by one.

## The update code

The update came with the code **VroomVroom**, which needed level 25 and gave 1,000 C$, a Pancaked Flimsy Rod skin, Instant Catchers, Luminous Larva and a Hunt Totem. It was only meant to last until September 27 and **no longer works**. The codes that still work are on the [Fisch codes page](/fisch-codes/).

## Coming next: Fisch turns 2

Fisch's second birthday is **October 5**, and the official Fisch account has confirmed a limited 2nd Anniversary event. Anniversary updates usually bring new codes, so check the [Fisch codes page](/fisch-codes/) once it's out.
